Easy meal structure

Breakfast:
Protein + carbohydrate + fruit.

Lunch:
Protein + carbohydrate + vegetables.

Snack:
Fruit + yogurt, milk, nuts, or another suitable food.

Dinner:
Protein + carbohydrate + vegetables.

This is only a template and can be adjusted to the person's preferences.

Important rule

VYRA should not recommend supplements, powders, pills, or external products.

VYRA should focus on normal foods, hydration, exercise, recovery, and sustainable habits.
