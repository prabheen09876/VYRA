Exercise Knowledge
What exercise does

Exercise helps improve strength, muscle, fitness, stamina, movement quality, and overall physical performance.

Different exercises have different purposes. A good training plan normally combines several types of movement instead of doing only one exercise.

The main types of exercise are:

Strength exercises
Muscle-building exercises
Cardio and running
Core exercises
Mobility exercises
Balance and coordination exercises
Recovery activities
Major muscle groups
Chest

Main movements:

Push-ups
Bench press
Dumbbell press
Incline press
Chest fly

Push-ups are a useful bodyweight option because they train the chest while also involving the shoulders, triceps, and core.

Back

Main movements:

Pull-ups
Assisted pull-ups
Rows
Lat pulldowns
Dumbbell rows

Back exercises help improve pulling strength and upper-body stability.

Shoulders

Main movements:

Overhead press
Dumbbell shoulder press
Lateral raises
Rear-delt raises
Arms

Biceps:

Curls
Hammer curls
Chin-ups

Triceps:

Push-ups
Close-grip push-ups
Dips
Triceps extensions
Legs

Quadriceps:

Squats
Split squats
Lunges
Step-ups

Glutes:

Squats
Hip thrusts
Glute bridges
Lunges
Split squats

Hamstrings:

Romanian deadlifts
Hamstring curls
Hip hinges

Calves:

Calf raises
Single-leg calf raises
Core

The core includes the muscles around the abdomen, sides, hips, and lower back.

Useful exercises:

Plank
Side plank
Dead bug
Bird dog
Leg raises
Controlled mountain climbers
Compound exercises

Compound exercises use several joints and muscle groups.

Examples:

Squat
Push-up
Pull-up
Row
Deadlift
Lunge
Overhead press

They are useful when a person wants to train many muscles efficiently.

Isolation exercises

Isolation exercises focus more strongly on one muscle or movement.

Examples:

Biceps curl
Triceps extension
Lateral raise
Calf raise
Leg curl

Isolation exercises can be added after larger movements.

Exercise selection

Choose exercises based on the person's:

Goal
Experience
Available equipment
Current strength
Mobility
Fitness level
Comfort with the movement

A beginner does not need advanced exercises.

A simple program can use:

Squat
Push-up
Row or pull movement
Hip-hinge movement
Core exercise
Walking or running
Bodyweight training

Bodyweight exercises are useful because they require little or no equipment.

Examples:

Squats
Push-ups
Lunges
Planks
Glute bridges
Step-ups
Mountain climbers

Exercises can be made easier or harder by changing:

Range of motion
Repetitions
Tempo
Rest time
Body position
Exercise variation
Exercise quality

Good exercise is not only about doing more repetitions.

Important factors include:

Good technique
Controlled movement
Appropriate range of motion
Consistency
Gradual progression
Recovery

If technique becomes poor because of fatigue, the person should reduce difficulty or rest.
