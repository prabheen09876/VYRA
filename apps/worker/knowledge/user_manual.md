# Using VYRA

## Getting started and choosing a goal

Create or restore your guest profile, then choose a fitness goal: build mass, reduce weight, or build strength while maintaining weight. Enter current height and weight, a target weight, and the starting build that best describes you. Starting build is self-described; the app does not diagnose you from body size.

Choose from Goku, Base Male, Base Female, Mikasa, Nami, and Sakura. A new player starts at Starter. Changing characters keeps your earned progress, goal, reports, and cosmetic ownership.

## Character evolution

Your character advances through Starter, Developing, Strong, and Elite. Evolution combines qualifying workouts, XP, distinct active days, and later check-ins toward your chosen target weight. Elite is the final earned stage. Reaching a weight target alone does not complete the workout requirements.

Developing requires 100 XP, 1 active day, and 25% target progress. Strong requires 1,000 XP, 5 active days, and 60%. Elite requires 3,000 XP, 14 active days, and 100%. For maintenance, a later report within 2% of target weight satisfies the weight part. Earned stages do not fall back because of normal fluctuations.

## Body check-ins

After setup, report current weight and height in your profile. Your first follow-up is available from the next day in Asia/Kolkata time. Updating the same day replaces that day's report. Weekly check-ins can help show trends; you do not need to enter measurements every day. Your starting baseline stays fixed.

Weight cannot measure muscle or fitness on its own. The reports support the goal you chose and should not encourage rapid weight changes.

## Arena opponent search and private battles

Open Multiplayer from the navigation or choose Play multiplayer on the home page. Find an opponent, create a private 1v1 room, or join a room with a code. Opponent search happens before camera preparation. Once both players are in the lobby, use Prepare my camera, allow camera access, get into position, and complete calibration before marking yourself ready.

Your friend may still be preparing their camera while you wait in the lobby. The normal workout flow uses real camera rep tracking, not simulated repetitions.

## Solo workouts and camera setup

Solo training prepares the camera before the workout. Use a supported browser or app, allow camera permission, give yourself enough room, and keep the required body joints visible in good lighting. Position the camera so the exercise can be seen clearly. Recalibrate if your position changes.

If tracking cannot see you clearly, improve the camera position or lighting before continuing. Leaving an active workout or moving the app into the background can end tracking; do not rely on an automatic pause-and-resume feature.

## Repetition counting and technique feedback

VYRA uses movement landmarks and calibration for supported camera exercises. Complete controlled repetitions and keep the relevant body joints visible. Tracking can miss a repetition when visibility or movement is incomplete. Camera feedback is a training aid, not a medical assessment or a guarantee of perfect technique.

Stop if an exercise causes sharp, severe, or unusual pain. Adjust the difficulty instead of forcing repetitions to satisfy the counter.

## Workout rewards

Finish a workout with at least 10 valid repetitions to qualify. The first three qualifying workouts per day earn XP and help build your active-day progress. Extra valid workouts can still contribute repetitions and battle wins to cosmetic unlocks. Previewing a character or cosmetic never earns rewards.

## Collection previews and character selection

Collection lets you preview all six characters and their four progression stages. A preview does not change your saved stage. Select a character to save it to your profile. Earned cosmetic effects can be equipped on every character; locked effects can be previewed before unlocking.

## Cosmetic unlocks, equip, remove, and original look

- Ion skin unlocks after your first qualifying workout.
- Pulse bracers unlock after 100 valid repetitions.
- Champion flex unlocks after 5 battle wins.
- Nova aura unlocks at Elite.

Equip an owned effect, remove it from its slot, or restore the character's original look to clear all equipped effects. Ownership, character selection, stage, XP, and fitness reports remain saved. Cosmetics change appearance and animation; they do not improve battle scoring or physical fitness.

## Coach questions and sources

Use the floating Ask Coach button to open chat over your current page. Ask about exercise technique, strength, muscle gain, running, stamina, recovery, everyday food choices, or how VYRA works. Minimize and reopen the chat to continue your conversation. Coach retrieves relevant passages from this bundled guide and shows the sources. When an AI provider is enabled, it can phrase an answer from those passages; otherwise you receive guide excerpts directly.

Your question and the recent conversation you submit may be sent to the configured AI provider. Profile measurements are not attached automatically. Source passages are general information and may not answer individual medical or nutrition questions.
