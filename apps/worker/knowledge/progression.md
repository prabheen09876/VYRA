# Training and Character Progression

## Gradual training progression

Progression means improving what your body can do over time. It can mean more controlled repetitions, more resistance, a harder variation, a larger comfortable range of motion, or more running time. It does not always mean adding weight.

Learn the movement first. Increase one training demand at a time only when you can perform the current level consistently with good technique. Increasing volume, intensity, frequency, and speed together makes recovery harder.

## Push-up progression

Possible steps are wall push-ups, incline push-ups, knee push-ups when appropriate, standard push-ups, close-grip push-ups, and decline push-ups. Choose a variation you can control. The exact sequence depends on your ability.

## Squat progression

Possible steps are bodyweight squats, controlled squats, pause squats, split squats, Bulgarian split squats, and loaded squats when appropriate. Comfortable depth, stable feet, and controlled movement come before more repetitions.

## Running progression

Start with walking, then walk/jog intervals, longer jogging periods, continuous running, and eventually longer or faster runs. Increase only one major demand at a time. VYRA's current camera workouts do not record running distance or pace.

## Four character stages and Elite requirements

VYRA's earned journey finishes at Elite: Starter, Developing, Strong, Elite. New players begin at Starter. Stage upgrades require both workout progress and progress toward the chosen target weight, confirmed by a later body check-in.

- Developing: 100 XP, 1 active day, and at least 25% of the way from starting weight to target weight.
- Strong: 1,000 XP, 5 active days, and at least 60% toward target weight.
- Elite: 3,000 XP, 14 active days, and reaching the chosen target weight.

For a maintenance goal, a later check-in within 2% of the chosen target weight satisfies the weight requirement. A weight report alone never grants XP or active days. Legendary is not an additional earned goal in this journey.

## Body check-ins and target weight

Choose a goal, starting build, height, current weight, target weight, and character during setup. Your starting measurements stay fixed as the baseline. Add height and weight reports from the next calendar day onward; days follow Asia/Kolkata time. A new report on the same day updates that day's report. Weekly check-ins are a useful rhythm; daily reporting is not required.

Character stages stay earned through normal weight fluctuations or missed reports. Self-reported weight is a goal metric, not a measurement of muscle gain or overall fitness. Training quality, performance, energy, recovery, and how you feel also matter.

## Workout XP and active days

A completed workout needs at least 10 valid repetitions to qualify. The first three qualifying workouts each day earn XP; further valid workouts can still add repetitions and wins for cosmetic unlocks. Active days count distinct days with a qualifying workout, not the number of workouts completed on one day.

## Workout and body progress together

If your character has not evolved, check all requirements: XP, distinct active days, a later body check-in, and progress toward your chosen target. For example, with a gain goal from 55 kg to 65 kg, 25% progress is 57.5 kg. Developing still also needs 100 XP and 1 active day. Do not rush weight changes to unlock a character stage.

## Tracking useful training progress

Record repetitions, manageable exercise variations, comfortable range of motion, and how well you recover. A training plateau may call for better sleep, easier sessions, technique practice, or more consistent training, rather than simply more volume. Previewing a stage or choosing a different character does not award progress.
