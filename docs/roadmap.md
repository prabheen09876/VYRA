# Product roadmap

The current release is a hackathon prototype. These milestones are planned work, not features silently claimed by the demo.

| Milestone | Work | Release evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reliable native beta | Apple signing, maintained native capture, expanded device matrix, recoverable accounts, reconnect, diagnostics, production character rigs/assets | Reproducible native builds; real-session reliability and privacy review; evaluated capture across target devices |
| Social game and collection | Friends, rematches, shareable character cards, more heroes/skins/body variants, achievements, challenges and seasons | Repeat-use evidence and fair cosmetic-only rewards; sharing and moderation controls |
| Personal fitness | Configurable goals, additional exercises and accessible variants, history, personalization, trained form scoring and evaluated pose tuning | More diverse consented datasets, movement-specific held-out evaluation, calibrated uncertainty and appropriate fitness review |
| Leagues and organizations | College, gym and workplace groups, tournaments, administration, supervised school experiences | Group admin/abuse controls, clear data access and retention, appropriate safeguarding for younger participants |
| Business and expansion | Validate premium cosmetics/analytics and organizational subscriptions; explore wearables, voice, GPS quests and AR | Retention and willingness-to-pay evidence before adding hardware/location complexity |

Before public distribution, replace prototype client trust with a measured anti-cheat strategy, strengthen rate limiting and account recovery, establish data retention/deletion, and review store/distribution requirements. Keep visual fantasy progression separate from body measurement and health claims. Evolution and rarity remain cosmetic.
